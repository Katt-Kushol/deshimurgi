Fulvous Whistling-Duck (Dendrocygna bicolor)

The fulvous whistling duck has a very large range extending across four continents. The Indian subcontinent is the Asian stronghold.[20] It undertakes seasonal movements in response to the availability of water and food. This species is usually found in small groups, but substantial flocks can form at favoured sites. It walks well, without waddling, and normally feeds by upending, though it can dive if necessary.
